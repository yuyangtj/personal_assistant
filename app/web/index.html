Memories

What the assistant remembers about you. Say “remember that …” or “forget …” in chat, or manage them here. Shared memories are given to the chat model with your messages (food ones to the grocery agent too). Private ones stay on your server and are never sent to any AI model; in chat, say “remember privately …”.

Purchases

Your Klarna purchases, from the exports you upload. Ask in chat, for example “what did I spend with Klarna last month?” or “how much at Uniqlo this year?”. Uploading a newer export adds new purchases and updates changed ones; nothing is duplicated. Stored on your server; a chat answer only uses the purchases it needs.
